The Watermelon Test

A quarterly report, the way the management board sees it. Everything in the green. The only question is: does it hold below the surface? Make your guess first, then we cut the melon open.

Your guess: how many of these four green indicators are actually red below the surface?

All four. Green on the outside, red on the inside, just like a watermelon. The lagging financial figures stay green long after the leading drivers have turned red. By the time the financial number flips, the decision window has usually already closed.

The didactic point: operational controlling watches the result (lagging). Strategic controlling watches the drivers (leading). More in the English slide deck and the research article draft.
